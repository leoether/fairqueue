# FairQueue Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026.

## What data FairQueue stores

FairQueue runs entirely inside Atlassian Jira Cloud on Atlassian Forge. It stores, in Forge app storage (KVS) on the Jira site where it is installed:

- Jira account IDs of team members shown in the workload panel;
- Assignment history: which issue was assigned to whom, and when;
- Workload statistics derived from the above (active issue counts, last assignment time).

## What FairQueue does NOT do

- It does not send data to any third-party server, analytics service, or advertiser.
- It does not sell or share personal data.
- It does not read issue content beyond what is needed to build the queue (issue key, status, priority).

## Retention and deletion

Data lives only in the Forge storage of the Jira site where the app is installed. When the app is uninstalled from a site, its stored data is removed with it.
